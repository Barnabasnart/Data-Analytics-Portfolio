# 🌍 Global Trade Solutions — Power BI & Executive Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI analytics solution evaluating global sales performance, order density, discount distributions, and category trends across 91 international customer accounts.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project processes cross-border transactional data to evaluate executive sales performance, category revenue contributions, and regional market concentration. It provides business leaders with actionable insights into purchasing volatility, high-performing product portfolios, and margin-preservation strategies.

*Business Objective:* Analyze global sales performance, evaluate category revenue drivers, and identify regional discount concentrations to optimize commercial strategy and revenue stability.

## 🎯 Key KPIs
* **Total Sales Revenue** — **$1.27M** cumulative revenue generated across 8 primary product categories.
* **Total Orders** — **2,155** completed orders spanning international markets.
* **Active Customer Base** — **91** unique global client accounts (Top client: *Brian Weiss* at $16.2K contribution).
* **Active Product Portfolio** — **77** active SKUs (Top leaders: *QDOMO*, *VJXYN*, and *UKXRI*).

## 📊 Dashboards

### 1. Executive Sales Overview
* Dynamic **Measure Selector** (disconnected table) to toggle between Total Revenue, Order Volume, and Discounted Units.
* Regional breakdown map and market footprint performance cards across top countries (**USA**, **Germany**, **Austria**).
* Slicers for **Product Category**, **Customer Segment**, and **Date Horizons**.
* Dynamic visual headers that update automatically based on the active sales metric.

### 2. Category & Market Analysis
* **Category Revenue Breakdown** — Performance comparison across all 8 product lines led by **Beverages** ($0.27M) and **Dairy Products** ($0.23M).
* **Discount Distribution Matrix** — Analysis highlighting regional discount volume concentration in the **USA** (60.17% / 17.98K units).
* **Purchasing Volatility Trends** — Monthly revenue tracking highlighting peak historical levels in **April 2008** followed by a sharp contraction in **May 2008**.

### 3. Details Tab
* Granular transaction matrix displaying individual order logs, customer details, and category line items.
* Drill-through configuration for localized country and customer-level performance exploration.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Power BI & DAX** — Advanced data modeling, custom measures, and interactive reporting.
* **Disconnected Tables** — Enables dynamic switching of metrics across primary visuals.
* **Bookmarks & Buttons** — Interactive UX features including filter resets and view navigation.
* **Conditional Formatting** — Highlighting category revenue leaders and discount thresholds.



## 🚀 Key Insights & Operational Outcomes
* **Q2 2008 Purchasing Volatility:** Monthly sales expanded significantly to peak historical levels in **April 2008**, followed by the sharpest single-month revenue contraction in **May 2008**, pointing toward potential supply chain bottlenecks or inventory depletion.
* **Category Performance Distribution:** **Beverages** ($0.27M) and **Dairy Products** ($0.23M) drive core revenue, while **Grains/Cereals** ($0.10M) and **Produce** ($0.10M) represent lagging opportunities requiring targeted bundling or pricing adjustments.
* **Geographic & Discount Concentration:** The **USA** is the company's primary market driver ($0.25M), but absorbs **60.17%** (17.98K units) of all global discounted sales, signaling an urgent need to audit discount structures to prevent margin erosion.
