# 🌐 Global Career Risk Assessment — Power BI & Predictive Analytics

An empirical data analysis evaluating Artificial Intelligence's macro and micro impacts on the global employment landscape leading up to 2030, covering 3,000 workers across 20 distinct job titles and 10 major industries.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes cross-border workforce dynamics, AI replacement risks, future demand projections, and salary distributions. It provides policymakers, workforce strategists, and professionals with actionable insights into changing capability floors, automation exposures, and career sustainability across sectors.

*Business Objective:* Evaluate workforce vulnerability and growth trajectories under accelerating AI automation to guide career planning, talent retention, and educational investments.

## 🎯 Key KPIs
* **Average Global Salary** — $126,641.75 across surveyed global roles.
* **Average Replacement Risk** — 50.3% mean AI automation/replacement probability.
* **Projected Job Growth** — +17.24% projected net employment growth leading up to 2030.
* **Average Future Demand** — 60.1% index score measuring long-term role sustainability.

## 📊 Dashboards

### 1. Overview Analysis
* Dynamic **Measure Selector** (disconnected table) to switch views between Replacement Risk, Future Demand Index, and Average Salary.
* Industry exposure breakdown across **Finance, Government, Healthcare, Tech, and Infrastructure**.
* Dynamic visual titles that update automatically based on the selected risk metric.
* Global slicers for Industry, Job Title, and Geographical Region.
* Matrix visual comparing risk scores vs. projected job growth across technical and non-technical domains.

### 2. Industry & Risk Analysis
* **High-Risk Concentrations** — Deep dive into top-exposure sectors like Finance (53.0% avg risk) and Government (52.9% avg risk).
* **Technical Infrastructure Paradox** — Visualization highlighting elevated automation risks in technical roles (e.g., DevOps, Cloud Architects) alongside strong future growth curves.
* Comparative breakdown of low-automation sectors like Healthcare.

### 3. Details Tab
* Detailed record matrix containing individual worker profile records and role vulnerability scores.
* Drill-through configuration for localized, role-specific risk exploration.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Power BI & DAX** — Data modeling, custom measures, and interactive reporting.
* **Disconnected Tables** — Enables dynamic switching of metrics across key visuals.
* **Bookmarks & Buttons** — Interactive UX features including filter resets and detail views.
* **Conditional Formatting** — Flagging high automation exposure and growth opportunities.

## 🚀 Key Insights & Outcomes
* **Industry Exposure:** Finance (53.0%) and Government (52.9%) lead in automated process exposure, whereas Healthcare exhibits the lowest risk profile.
* **Infrastructure Shifts:** Technical infrastructure positions face script remediation exposure but maintain robust job growth indexes, signaling a shifting capability floor rather than net job loss.
* **Career Adaptation:** Provides a framework for identifying high-demand, low-risk career transitions leading into 2030.
